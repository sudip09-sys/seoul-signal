YOUR SYLLABLE가

A block, not a string.

ㄱ + ㅏ = 가 · Vertical vowels sit to the right. Horizontal vowels sit underneath: ㄱ + ㅗ = 고. A final consonant, called 받침, goes below: 가 + ㄴ = 간.

Initial ㅇ is silent: ㅇ + ㅏ = 아. At the end of a syllable, ㅇ sounds like “ng.” Romanization is a starting aid; listen to the Korean and learn the letters.
